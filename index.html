What If I Invested?

What is this website?

What If I Invested? is a website that lets you explore what could have happened if you invested money into different cryptocurrencies in the past. Using past cryptocurrency price data from CoinGecko, the website calculates how much your investment could have increased or decreased over a selected period of time.

How to use it

Choose a cryptocurrency from the navigation bar above. Enter the amount you would have initially invested and select the duration you would have kept the investment. The website will then use past price data to calculate the hypothetical profit or loss and display the price history on a graph.
