Bitcoin

Bitcoin is a digital currency created in 2008 by a person or group using the name Satoshi Nakamoto. It was made to let people send money directly to each other without needing a bank or government to control it. Bitcoin uses blockchain technology to record transactions and there will only ever be 21 million Bitcoin.
